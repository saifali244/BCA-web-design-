Traditional Frames and Framesets

Older HTML used the <frameset> element to divide a browser window into separate frames. The <frame> element loaded a different HTML document inside each frame.

Limitations of Frames

  • Framesets are obsolete in HTML5.
  • They can make navigation, bookmarking and printing confusing.
  • Accessibility and responsive layouts can be difficult.
  • Individual frames may not work well on small screens.
